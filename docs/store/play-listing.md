## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
QuaDev is the home of our AI-powered tools for professionals. This first release sets up your QuaDev account, so you are ready as soon as new features arrive.

With QuaDev you can:
- Create an account with your email address, or sign in with Google.
- Verify your email and reset a forgotten password securely.
- Set up your profile: your name and, if you want, your date of birth.
- Delete your account and its data at any time, directly in the app.

No ads, no tracking. We only keep what is needed to run your account, and crash reports that help us fix problems.
```
